Dwarf Trade Automation

DTA is DwarfX's universal market-analysis software: it reads the visible chart context and turns that context into structured, explainable signals.

Product direction
  • Chart-aware — Work from the chart the user is actually viewing, rather than locking the product to one market.
  • Explainable signals — Buy and sell tags are paired with the chart conditions that produced them.
  • Flexible input — Manual asset or ticker input can complement visual chart and timeframe recognition.
  • Focused overlay — Keep analysis available beside the chart without covering the user's workspace.
